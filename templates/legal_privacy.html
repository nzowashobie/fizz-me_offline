Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account: username, phone number, password (stored in protected form), and optional profile photo.
  • Messages: chats, attachments, and status posts you send.
  • Deals: listings, offers, deal records, delivery steps, ratings, and payment references you enter.
  • Payment proof: screenshots and reference numbers you upload to show a payment was made.
  • Contacts: phone numbers you choose to check against Fizzme. Numbers you do not add are not kept.
  • Technical data: device and browser type, and basic usage logs needed to keep the service secure.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, deliver messages, show listings, and track deals.
  • To find friends by phone number, matching only numbers that already have an account.
  • To send notifications you have allowed.
  • To prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

3. Who can see your information

  • Other users can see your username, profile photo, listings, and the messages and deal details you share with them.
  • Payment screenshots and references are visible only to the buyer and seller of that deal.
  • Service providers that host the app and store media process data for us, under contracts that protect it.
  • Authorities may receive information where the law requires it.

4. Keeping your information

We keep your information while your account is active. Deal and payment records are kept for a period needed to resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Payment screenshots are removed when they are no longer needed for a deal.

5. Security

Fizzme uses HTTPS for data in transit, restricts access to stored files, and keeps payment proof private. No system is completely secure, so keep your password safe and tell us if you think your account has been used without permission.

6. Your rights

Under the Zambia Data Protection Act, 2021, you can ask to see, correct, or delete your personal information, and to object to some uses of it. To make a request, contact us using the address below.

7. Children

Fizzme is for people aged 18 and over. Do not create an account if you are younger.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app.
